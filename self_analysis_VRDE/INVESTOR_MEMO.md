# INVESTOR MEMO — Verde Kitchen (VRDE)

**Analyst:** Bianca
**Date:** 2026-09-07
**Recommendation:** SHORT into the print, expressed via puts
**Conviction:** Medium (signal is strong and corroborated; main uncertainty is whether it is already priced)

---

### 1. Situation
VRDE reports Q2 earnings in ~1 week. It is a premium-multiple growth stock (~55x)
priced for durable same-store sales (comps) plus rapid unit growth. The question:
does the alt-data package give us a leading read on comps ahead of the print?

### 2. The data
- **Card panel** (weekly, split into new vs existing customer cohorts, national).
- **Web/app** (downloads, weekly active users, sessions per user).
- **Job postings** (total + new-location hiring).
- **Street context** (consensus, comp history, valuation).

Key limitations, stated up front:
- It is a **panel (a sample)** — trust *trends*, never the absolute dollar level.
- **No geography split** — cannot test regional store dynamics (a data-ask for the vendor).
- **Panel coverage stepped up ~15% at week 18** (vendor added a bank). Neutralized by
  reading **per-active-card** metrics, which are immune to panel size.

### 3. The signal
Decomposing the existing-customer cohort (the same-store proxy):
- **Frequency (visits/card): Q1 avg 0.927 -> Q2 avg 0.896, -3.4%.** Same-store demand
  stepped down and then plateaued at the lower level. Not a collapse, a reset.
- **Ticket roughly flat** (mild price inflation), so the softness is real *visit* demand,
  not a pricing artifact. Frequency is the cleanest read and it is deteriorating.
- Coverage jump handled: per-card metrics did not move on the week-18 step, so the
  deceleration is genuine, not an artifact.

### 4. Cross-checks (triangulation)
- **App engagement (sessions/WAU) is declining ~6-7%** — an *independent* dataset
  confirming the same softening. Orthogonal corroboration raises conviction materially.
- **New-location job postings ~tripled** — the growth is **unit-driven** (opening doors),
  which explains away the one bullish-looking number (the new-customer surge).
- Revenue ~= unit growth + comps. Units (+15%) are disclosed and already priced; the
  **entire edge is the comp read**, and the panel implies comps are tracking **below the
  +6% consensus**.

### 5. The edge
- **Differentiated:** requires decomposing the panel by cohort *and* cleaning the coverage
  trap — work most of the market skips, so they see a healthy headline.
- **Timely:** a leading read on a **dated catalyst** (the print in ~1 week).
- **Not-yet-priced:** consensus comps are still +6%; our data says lower.
- **Why the edge exists:** the market is anchored on the headline and the unit-growth
  story; the same-store deterioration is only visible underneath, in the existing cohort.

### 6. Risks / what would make me wrong
- **Already priced in (the #1 risk):** card panels are widely subscribed. TEST before
  trading — is the stock already selling off, is put skew rich, is short interest high,
  has it lagged peers? If yes, edge is spent and puts are expensive.
- **Panel not representative (selection bias):** if the panel over-indexes weak regions,
  it may overstate the national comp softness (ties to the no-geography limitation).
- **Headline saves it:** revenue beats on units and the market forgives soft comps.
- **Management offsets:** guide raise, buyback, or new-market announcement on print day.
- **Options math:** puts are priced for a move; the actual move must exceed the implied.

**Compliance gate (must clear before ANY trade):**
provenance / MNPI (aggregated, anonymized, no insider leakage), consumer consent
(GDPR/CCPA), and licensing (right to *trade* not just research, chain of title, no
competitor exclusivity); web-scrape ToS/CFAA for the app feed. Compliance is a gate,
not a scoring dimension: great alpha + dirty provenance = un-investable.

### 7. Recommendation
**Short VRDE via put options into the Q2 print**, sized modestly for defined risk,
**contingent on the crowding check** in Section 6. Catalyst: Q2 comps + guide, ~1 week.
Thesis: headline revenue may roughly hit on unit growth, but comps miss the +6%
consensus and the FY comp guide is cut; at 55x that forces a multiple de-rate.
